Forecast is forward-looking: revenue and cost are spread across months at the contract's daily rate, and finished months are excluded so delivered work is not counted as forecast. The current month always counts in full. An in-flight project contributes its remaining pro-rata share; one that ended before this month contributes nothing. By project & deal is not a forecast — it reports whole-contract revenue and margin over the full HubSpot date range, ignores the month range, and includes finished engagements. Projects are work already won; deal stages are open pipeline shown at full value, not probability-weighted. Scoping deals with no dollar value are excluded from the forecast and listed under Data gaps. P&L is actuals only — no forecast and no HubSpot data in it. Sources: HubSpot Projects and Sales Pipeline deals for the forecast; QuickBooks Online for the P&L.
